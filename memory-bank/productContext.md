# Product Context - Uganda Economic Dashboard

## Problem Statement
Uganda's economic data is often scattered across different sources, presented in complex formats that are difficult for various stakeholders to understand and analyze. There's a need for a centralized, visual platform that makes economic indicators accessible and actionable.

## Why This Project Exists
- **Data Accessibility**: Economic data should be available to all citizens, not just economists
- **Transparency**: Open visualization of economic progress builds public trust
- **Decision Support**: Visual data helps inform policy and investment decisions
- **Progress Tracking**: Clear metrics help track national development goals
- **Community Engagement**: Open source approach builds local tech capacity

## Target Users & Use Cases

### Primary Users
1. **Policy Makers**
   - Need: Quick access to current economic indicators
   - Use: Strategic planning and policy development
   - Value: Real-time insights for informed decision making

2. **Economic Researchers**
   - Need: Historical trends and growth projections
   - Use: Academic research and analysis
   - Value: Comprehensive data visualization tools

3. **Citizens & Media**
   - Need: Understandable economic information
   - Use: Civic engagement and informed discourse
   - Value: Simplified, visual presentation of complex data

4. **International Partners**
   - Need: Uganda's economic progress monitoring
   - Use: Investment and aid decision making
   - Value: Standardized, accessible reporting

## Key Economic Focus Areas

### Current Metrics Displayed
- **GDP Tracking**: Total economic output and growth rates
- **Population Dynamics**: Population size and growth trends
- **Per Capita Analysis**: Individual economic indicators
- **Growth Projections**: Future economic forecasting
- **Middle Income Progress**: Path to middle-income country status

### Economic Context (Default Data)
- **Current GDP**: $34 billion (6.3% growth rate)
- **Population**: 42.86 million (3.3% growth rate)
- **Target**: Lower Middle Income status ($1,025 per capita)

## User Experience Goals
1. **Immediate Understanding**: Users should grasp key metrics within seconds
2. **Interactive Exploration**: Ability to explore different economic scenarios
3. **Mobile Accessibility**: Works across all device types
4. **Data Input Flexibility**: Users can input custom economic scenarios
5. **Visual Clarity**: Clean, professional presentation suitable for reports

## Success Metrics
- **Usage**: Regular visits from target user groups
- **Engagement**: Time spent exploring different economic scenarios
- **Contributions**: Active community participation in development
- **Accuracy**: Reliable, up-to-date economic data representation
- **Impact**: Referenced in policy discussions and research

## Community Value
The dashboard serves as both a practical tool and a demonstration of Uganda's growing tech capabilities, fostering:
- Open source development skills
- Data visualization expertise
- Economic literacy in the community
- Collaborative government-citizen engagement

This product bridges the gap between complex economic data and public understanding, empowering informed participation in Uganda's economic development.
